About Jan Setu

Empowering Every Citizen

Jan Setu bridges the gap between citizens and government. A transparent digital-first channel where every voice matters and every complaint is tracked to resolution.

New: Our City Issues Voting feature lets citizens collectively signal which problems affect the most people — across all cities — pushing government to act on real priorities.

Democratic Voting

Citizens upvote real problems. The most-voted issues rise to the top, giving government clear data on what needs fixing first.

🔒

Secure & Private

Phone numbers are hashed — we prevent double-voting while protecting your identity.

Real-time Notifications

SMS and email updates at every stage — from submission to resolution.
